Caveats (read before comparing)

  • Decay dates differ: Hanford BBI decayed to 2022-01-01; SRS PA values decayed to 2032. The site-level current difference (191 vs ~123 MCi) is dominated by real inventory, not decay.
  • SRS per-tank = closure residuals: what will remain AFTER waste removal (4,000 gal/tank assigned basis). NOT today's tank contents. HTF values are conservative max-of-group assignments.
  • Hanford totals include all 177 tanks incl. inactive single-shell farms; both sites' totals shrink as processing continues (SRS: 91% of curies in salt waste targeted by SWPF/DWPF; Hanford: DFLAW vitrification + TSCR).
  • Chemical bases differ: SRS per-tank kg are elements on residual sludge; Hanford kg include supernate salts (Na nitrate/nitrite dominate mass). The Current Chemistry tab gives the fair waste-type comparison.
  • Ba-137m / Y-90 are daughters counted with parents at both sites — treat as families (Cs-137+Ba-137m, Sr-90+Y-90).
