Unsupervised Cross-Site Waste Taxonomy — Hanford BBI vs SRS SRNL samples

Two-stage unsupervised learning: Hanford's 433 tank × phase Best-Basis Inventory compositions define a waste taxonomy per feature space (Ward hierarchical clustering on Aitchison/CLR-transformed compositions); SRS's measured DWPF/SWPF samples and closure-residual vectors are then projected onto that taxonomy with shared-support Aitchison distances. Scale-invariant CLR geometry makes mg/L, µg/g, kg and Ci/gal directly comparable without density assumptions.
